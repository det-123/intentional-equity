What we do

Five areas of practice, each designed to leave your HR function stronger and more self-sufficient when the engagement ends.

HR Function Optimization

We look at how HR work actually moves through your organization: who owns what, where requests stall, and where time goes. Then we redesign the workflows, roles, and tools so your HR function runs with less friction and more consistency.

  • HR department assessments
  • Workflow and process mapping
  • Workload and capacity analysis
  • SOPs, playbooks, and trackers
  • HR reporting
  • HR technology review

HR Team Development

A strong HR function depends on capable people. We develop in-house HR staff through regular working sessions, guidance on live cases, and the tools they need to handle more on their own. Coaching is delivered by ICF-credentialed coaches.

  • Coaching for HR generalists and new HR leaders
  • Case-based guidance
  • Compliance and employee relations skill-building
  • A clear path to independence

HR Compliance & Risk

Requirements change constantly, and a misstep is costly. We keep policies, practices, and people decisions on solid ground, and we step in quickly when a situation is urgent.

  • Leave administration (FMLA, ADA, state leave)
  • FLSA classification
  • Benefit and retirement plan compliance
  • Multi-state employment compliance
  • Handbook and policy reviews
  • Employee relations and investigations
  • Separations and transitions

Fractional & Interim HR Leadership

Senior HR leadership when you need it: to bridge a gap while you recruit, to put an assessment's recommendations into action, or to lead HR until you are ready for a full-time hire.

  • Interim HR leadership
  • Assessment implementation
  • Executive and manager advisory
  • Building HR from the ground up

Workplace Equity & Inclusion

Practical, compliance-grounded work that makes how you hire, pay, and advance people consistent, transparent, and defensible.

  • Pay equity analysis
  • Equitable policy and practice review
  • Structured, inclusive hiring practices
  • Promotion and advancement criteria
  • Manager education on fair, consistent decisions

How we work

Engagements are structured around what your situation requires, whether that is a single decision, a defined project, or senior support over time.

Focused advisory

Experienced guidance on a specific question or decision, such as a high-risk employee situation, a compliance question, or a separation.

Project engagements

A defined scope with set deliverables, milestones, and an implementation plan.

Ongoing advisory

A steady partnership with continuity and context, structured on a regular cadence alongside your leadership or HR team.

Fractional leadership

Embedded senior HR leadership, combining strategy with hands-on execution, for as long as you need it.

What to expect

  1. Conversation

    We learn about your organization, your people, and what prompted you to reach out.

  2. Assessment and scope

    We review what is in place and confirm scope, deliverables, timeline, and fees in writing.

  3. Delivery

    We do the work alongside your team, with regular check-ins and no surprises.

  4. Handoff

    You receive finished tools and documentation your team can use and maintain.

Every engagement begins with an introductory conversation at no cost. Scope and fees are then tailored to the complexity, timeline, and level of support your organization needs.

About the firm

We believe organizations are stronger when people decisions are made on purpose, applied consistently, and able to withstand a hard question.

Intentional Equity was founded in 2023. To us, equity means fairness in practice: the same standards applied to the same situations, people paid fairly for the work they do, and HR practices that hold up when someone asks why.

We approach HR as an honest broker. Our role is to protect the organization, respect the employee, and bring clarity to the employment relationship. We treat compliance as part of managing risk, and we build infrastructure your team can sustain long after an engagement ends.

Engagements are led by senior consultants with more than 15 years of HR experience and SHRM-SCP, SPHR, and ICF credentials.
